# Results Summary

## 1. Documented Missense Mutation (c.518T>A)
* **Type:** Missense substitution.
* **Effect:** Produces a full-length protein (495 amino acids), but substitutes Isoleucine with Asparagine at position 173 (I173N).

## 2. Artificial Single-Nucleotide Deletion (del518)
* **Type:** Frameshift deletion.
* **Effect:** Deletes 1 base pair at position 518, shifting the reading frame from position 173 onward. This alters the downstream amino acid sequence and introduces premature stop codons, leading to a truncated protein.
